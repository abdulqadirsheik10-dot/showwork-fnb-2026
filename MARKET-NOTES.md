# Market evidence and assumptions

Sources checked 25 October 2026. This is secondary research. No simulated responses are presented as observations or customers.

## Need

Statistics South Africa reports youth unemployment of 47.4% for ages 15-34 in Q2 2026, with five million unemployed youth. This is the unemployment rate within the youth labour force, not a share of all youth. [Stats SA release](https://www.statssa.gov.za/?p=19804).

The NYDA stresses that youth unemployment reflects a structural shortage of employment opportunities. Practice tools alone cannot solve it. [NYDA response](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/nyda-notes-serious-concern-latest-quarterly-labour-force-survey-qlfs-q2-12).

ShowWork tests a narrower hypothesis: a short, contextual work sample may help a first-time retail applicant practise and give a reviewer more useful evidence than an unsupported CV claim. The macro statistic does not validate this mechanism.

## Existing offerings

- [Forage](https://www.theforage.com/) provides free, self-paced job simulations and model answers.
- [SA Youth / Harambee](https://www.harambee.co.za/work-seekers/) connects work seekers with opportunities. Its [employer offering](https://www.harambee.co.za/employers/) includes matching based on geography, education and requirements.
- [TestGorilla](https://www.testgorilla.com/assessments/) offers tests, simulations and other hiring assessment tools. Its [candidate profiles](https://candidates.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/33780126911899-Taking-a-skills-test-on-your-TestGorilla-Profile) also support self-service tests. It would be wrong to claim that candidates cannot independently take tests on this platform.

ShowWork's proposed focus is short retail practice, visible revisions and user-controlled work sharing. This is a product choice, not proof of an exclusive feature or an unserved market. Larger products have much broader scope, employer relationships and established content. Copied or AI-written responses are also an unresolved authorship risk, so ShowWork makes no verified-skill claim.

## Commercial test proposed

Free candidate practice and sharing. First potential payer: youth training providers, with future cohort tools at a hypothetical R1,500/month. No paid cohort features are built and willingness to pay is untested. Employers are initially reviewers.

Illustrative monthly operating allowance: hosting R500; future AI R500; monitoring/domain allocation R200; support R500; contingency R300. Total R2,000 before founder time, salaries, tax, acquisition and legal review. These are allowances, not current spending or vendor quotes. Two provider subscriptions would yield R3,000 gross; this arithmetic does not establish profitability or demand.

## Next validation

Proposed pilot, not completed: 50 candidates, five retail reviewers, target 60% task completion. Observe whether users can complete the flow without help; have people review original/revised work independently; ask reviewers about usefulness and providers about payment. Evaluate authorship, accessibility, language and device barriers. A phrase-match count is not evidence of improved ability. No jobs-created, partnership or primary-research claim is made.
